Day List Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Day List is a personal to-do app. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Signing in

When you sign in with Google, Day List receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. It uses them only to identify your account and show who is signed in.

Your tasks

Tasks you create are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account. Security rules allow only you, while signed in, to read or change them. The demo mode stores sample tasks only in your own browser.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Day List asks for read-only access to your calendar so it can show your appointments next to your tasks. It never creates, changes or deletes calendar events. Appointment details are kept only in your browser on your own device, so they can be shown when you are offline. They are never sent to any server other than Google's. You can disconnect at any time from the account menu, or remove access in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.

Day List's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Day List does not sell, share or use your information for advertising, and has no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual tasks in the app, or export everything as a backup file from the account menu. To have your whole account's data deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
